Limitations

What our results do not tell you

Synthetic voters

Tuned so the game is playable, not fitted to a real electorate.

One party

No opponent, the manifesto is fixed, friendships never change.

Heuristic baselines

The Oracle and the plan search are strong, but not proven optimal.

Sampled knowledge

Logic verdicts come from a finite sample of smaller worlds.

No human data

Every result comes from computer players; the playtest was dropped.
