# DEC-012 — Observation profile experiment proposal

**Status: APPROVED.**  
**Decision owner:** project team (all five reviewers).  
**Date proposed:** 2026-10-23.

Keep `Observation` schema v1 aggregate-only. Do not disclose voter-level support, susceptibility, hidden graph edges, or hidden seeds in the core API. The Week 5 information-profile experiment remains blocked until the team approves exact fields, sampling semantics, versioning, privacy boundaries, and reviewer sign-off in a subsequent decision-log entry. This proposal authorizes no disclosure implementation and no information-level result claims.

Rationale: DEC-007 is frozen; proposed information percentages in the Week 5 plan are not operationally defined. Approval of Week 5 implementation is not implicit approval to widen the public information boundary.
